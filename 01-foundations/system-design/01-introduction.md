## What is System Design?
- System Design is the process of defining the architechture, components, modules, interfaces and data flow for a system to meet specific requirements. [Functional Requirement]
- It Involves making high level decisions about scalibility, reliability, performance, and maintainability. [Non Functional Requirement]
- Used in real-world applicaitons like designing scalable web apps, distributed systems, databases and cloud infrastructres.

## Why System Design is Important?
- Scalability & Reliability - Ensures systems handle millions of users without failure.
- Architectural Thinking - Goes beyond coding; involves tradeoffs like CAP theorem, SQL vs NoSQL,  Microservices vs Monolith
- Career Growth - Essential for becoming a senior engineer or architect.
- Real-World Problem Solving - Helps in building actual systems, not just clearing interviews.
- Trade-Offs & Desicion Making - Balances scalability, cost, speed and complexity.
- Future-Proofing - Prevents bottlenecks and allows smooth evolution of software.

## The Evolution of System Design Over the last 25 Yeards
- 1995-2005: Early Monolithic Web Applications
- 2005-2010: Scaling Challenges & Distributed Systems
- 2010-2015: The Cloud Revolution
- 2015-2020: Microservices & Event Driven Architectures
- 2020-Present: Real-Time, AI Driven& Edge Computing
